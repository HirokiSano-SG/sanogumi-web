Publishing Inquiries

Sano-Gumi Co., Ltd. provides publishing for games by individuals and small teams on Steam and other stores. This is not employment or full-time recruitment. We work with you as an external partner on a per-title basis.

Our policy is simple: a game made with joy is the strongest. We protect the creator's discretion and don't interfere unnecessarily. If we have concerns we will speak up, but only as suggestions — we won't force anything.

What we can do

  • Support for store submissions, builds, store pages, and operations before and after release
  • Supported stores (examples): Steam. On request, we also support Epic Games Store (EOS), Microsoft Store, and PlayStation
  • A sounding board (our representative, who has experience with commercial titles; the author makes the final decisions)
  • Development-side work such as porting and migration (with a larger revenue share accordingly)

What to submit when applying

The following two items are required.

  • A proposal that can be read in about 3–5 minutes (longer is fine)
  • A playable pre-production build (graphics, tutorials, etc. can be unfinished — just something that shows the core concept)

If you have anything else to show us, feel free to include it (trailers, past works, social media, technical notes — any format).

What happens next

After reviewing your submission, if we are interested we will set up an interview and a Q&A. A guideline for the terms will be put in writing based on the title and the amount of work.

Who this suits

  • You want to keep creative control of your work
  • You want a publisher to “help you deliver” rather than “fix” your game
  • You want to outsource platform porting or cleaning up technical debt

Who this doesn't suit

  • You want the publisher to make the final decisions on the concept and expression
  • You are looking only for a fixed one-time buyout (we work mainly on revenue share)

To start, please send us the items above along with what you're currently struggling with.
