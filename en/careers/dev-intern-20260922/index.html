Game Development Members Wanted (Revenue Share, Independent Contractor)

This opening is not employment. It is a revenue-share independent-contractor arrangement (a revenue-share participation agreement and a non-disclosure agreement). There is no hourly wage, fixed fee, or minimum guarantee. You receive a share of the relevant title's sales according to the deliverables you take on. You decide when and where you work. If a title makes no sales, or is released for free, there may be nothing to share.

Students may apply on the same contractor terms. If you want an employment-based internship for a school practicum or credits, see Student Internship & Hourly Part-Time (Employment).

Please read before applying: all communication is in Japanese.

  • Japanese ability: JLPT N2 level or above. Messages, specifications, and meetings are all in Japanese, and you should be able to communicate in written Japanese on Discord without difficulty. Our representative cannot communicate in English.
  • Residence status: if you live in Japan, you need a residence status that permits you to receive compensation (for international students, e.g., permission to engage in activities outside their status). People living outside Japan may also apply.
  • Please write your application email in Japanese. Our replies and application guidance will also be sent in Japanese.

What we are recruiting for

We are still recruiting people to join as revenue-share independent contractors. This is not an hourly wage or a fixed fee. You receive a share of the sales of the games we make, according to the deliverables you take on. We think it suits people who say, “I have a main job, but I want to be deeply involved in making games,” or “I want to join a project where my name appears in the credits.”

Current projects at Sano-Gumi include “Dreamy Sakura's Graduation Ceremony” (releasing on Steam on October 22), “ぱずホロ” (a fan-made work based on the hololive production Derivative Works Guidelines, in development with the aim of applying to holo Indie; © 2016 COVER Corp.), and “Don't Give Up, Pal” (がんばれ相棒). We have other projects in progress as well. Anyone who can use Unity, draw, or make sound is welcome. The main role is engineering, but you can also join as an artist (see “For those who want to join as an artist” below). It's fine to work using AI agents.

If you are interested, please read the conditions on this page, or feel free to send a DM on X (@ahTama). Once you have made up your mind, please send an email to the application address below. Messages and applications should be written in Japanese.

For those who want to join as an artist

Until now the main role has been engineering, but you can also join as an artist. The arrangement is a revenue-share independent contract.

We accept UI, animation, effects, and character design. Our representative will give you specific comments and feedback on what you make.

For illustration, we can only accept people whose current skill level is clear from their portfolio. We will give comments and feedback on your work, but we are sorry that we cannot teach drawing from the basics or offer consultation on personal concerns as an illustrator. Thank you for your understanding.

People who join as engineers can also get involved in UI and effects work.

If you wish to join as an artist, please include your portfolio (a URL is fine) with your application.

For applicants of foreign nationality

Nationality does not matter.

However, all communication, specifications, and meetings are in Japanese. Our representative cannot communicate in English. As a guide, we expect Japanese at the level of JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test) N2 or above, and that you can communicate in written Japanese on Discord without difficulty.

Eligible applicants are those who live outside Japan, or those who live in Japan and whose residence status permits them to receive compensation (for international students, e.g., permission to engage in activities outside their status).

Work we ask you to take on (contracted work)

We ask you to develop our own games and handle the practical store work. Not only Steam, but also iOS, Android, and Nintendo Switch. That includes store applications, builds, and submission. The first Steam work will be done together with our representative; from then on, the goal is for you to be able to handle it on your own. How the work is divided, and details of the titles, will be explained in a meeting.

Who we welcome

This is an opportunity for people who want to grow. The goal is to become an engineer who can work independently. We are looking for people who do not stop at waiting for instructions, and who can take the work on as their own. People who are not like this are not eligible.

Working efficiently with AI agents is required. If you are not sure how to use them, we will share a starting point for how to proceed. You can apply even if you have not used them before. People who will not use them, or who refuse to use them, are not eligible.

In addition to Steam, this work involves iOS, Android, and Nintendo Switch. People who do not want to work on multiple platforms are not eligible.

For people who want to put their own games on stores in the future, we hope you will use this as a stepping stone toward that goal.

Conditions

Type of arrangement
This is a revenue-share independent contract. We sign a revenue-share participation agreement and a non-disclosure agreement. It is not an employment contract. There is no hourly wage, fixed fee, or minimum guarantee.
Time
You decide when you work. Deadlines for deliverables (milestones) and how progress is shared follow what the contract agrees. As a guide, we share progress on Discord about once a week.
Location
Both meetings and the work are fully remote (online). Communication via Discord is required. Slack is used only for those who want it.
Equipment
Please use your own computer. The company does not lend equipment.
Confidentiality
Once you are accepted, we will sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA). This is mandatory.
Contract
This is an independent contract (a revenue-share participation agreement). Because it is not an employment contract, it is outside minimum wage, overtime pay, social insurance, and workers' accident compensation insurance. You may be able to use the special workers' accident insurance enrollment for freelancers on your own. Details are settled in the meeting and the contract. If agreed deadlines are missed repeatedly, or contact stops, we may end the contract. The share percentage may be revised according to the deliverables you actually took on.
Revenue sharing
This is for people who can work almost independently. It takes the form of splitting off part of our representative's (Hiroki Sano's) share. The percentage, target titles, and payment terms are set individually in the contract. Payment is not based on hours worked. It is a share of the relevant title's sales, according to the deliverables you took on. If there are no sales, or the title is released for free, the share can be zero yen. We do the first Steam work together, a progress check about once a week, a sounding board when you get stuck, and a review before submission. That does not include daily step-by-step instructions or staying with you the whole time.
Name
For public-facing names, chat, credits, and so on, a handle name is fine. Your real name is required for the contract, bank transfers, and taxes.
My Number (Japanese individual number)
Not needed for the application or the meeting. If it becomes necessary for payment procedures, we will let you know after the contract.
How to apply
Please send an email in Japanese to rin-sano@sanogumi.biz saying that you want a revenue-share independent contract (if you are applying as an artist, include your portfolio — a URL is fine), along with your name (a handle name is fine), contact details, a time of day when you are easy to reach, and a brief summary of your achievements or what you are studying. Our replies and application guidance will also be sent in Japanese.
