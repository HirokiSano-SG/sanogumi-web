Student Internship & Hourly Part-Time (Employment)

This page is about hourly part-time work under an employment contract, and internships for a school practicum or credits. The hourly wage is at or above the minimum wage. It is separate from the revenue-share independent contract.

If you want to join on a revenue-share independent contract, see Game Development Members Wanted (Revenue Share, Independent Contractor).

Please read before applying: all communication is in Japanese.

  • Japanese ability: JLPT N2 level or above. Messages, specifications, and meetings are all in Japanese, and you should be able to communicate in written Japanese on Discord without difficulty. Our representative cannot communicate in English.
  • Residence status: if you live in Japan, you need a residence status that permits you to work and receive compensation (for international students, e.g., permission to engage in activities outside their status). People living outside Japan may also apply.
  • Please write your email in Japanese. Our replies will also be sent in Japanese.

Recruitment status (updated September 28, 2026)

Hourly part-time positions (including internships with practical work) are closed because they are full. Thankfully, the places were filled. To everyone who applied, thank you very much. We were able to talk with some of you in meetings, and everyone was wonderful — honestly, it was a very hard decision.

We are not accepting new applications at this time.

This is an employment contract

This opening is an employment contract. Pay is an hourly wage, at or above the minimum wage. Details are settled in the interview and the contract.

For students (school practicum or credits)

If you want this to count as a school practicum or for credits, Sano-Gumi will write certificates and evaluations for the practicum. Each school has its own conditions, such as a supervisor or time records, so please tell us your school's rules when you apply.

This is not an observation-only internship. We ask you to do actual work.

Applicants under 20 need a letter of consent from a parent or guardian. As with all other participants, signing a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and communicating via Discord are required (Slack only for those who want it).

For applicants of foreign nationality

Nationality does not matter.

However, all communication, specifications, and meetings are in Japanese. Our representative cannot communicate in English. As a guide, we expect Japanese at the level of JLPT (Japanese-Language Proficiency Test) N2 or above, and that you can communicate in written Japanese on Discord without difficulty.

Eligible applicants are those who live outside Japan, or those who live in Japan and whose residence status permits them to receive compensation (for international students, e.g., permission to engage in activities outside their status).

Conditions

Type of position
Hourly part-time work under an employment contract, and internships with practical work. The hourly wage is at or above the minimum wage. Recruitment is closed because the positions are full.
Hours
The standard was 3 hours a day, 3 to 5 days a week. Days of up to 7 hours were limited to 2 per week, and days of 8 hours or more were not available. This was because our representative cannot always be present.
Location
Both interviews and work are fully remote (online). Communication via Discord is required. Slack is used only for those who want it.
Equipment
Please use your own computer for work. The company does not lend equipment.
Confidentiality
Once you are accepted, we will sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA). This is mandatory.
Name
For public-facing names, chat, credits, and so on, a handle name is fine. Your real name is required for the contract, bank transfers, and taxes.
My Number (Japanese individual number)
Not needed for the application or interview. If it becomes necessary for payment procedures, we will let you know after the contract.
Contact
Recruitment is closed. Questions can be sent to rin-sano@sanogumi.biz.
